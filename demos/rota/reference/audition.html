Boot chime audition

Three voices over I–vi–IV–ii–V–I in D. A rising figure passes from the lead to the harmony to the bass, a beat apart; then the lead turns it upside down and the harmony answers the same way, and the lead settles on F♯5 while the bass plays the figure once more into the crash, over one roll through the V and I chords. Each voice has a thread in its colour that spins out when it plays the figure and flips when it inverts.
