Rota tile animation lab

The splash for tile hardware. The ring, lambdas and letters are drawn once while the screen is dark; a fabric of traces fills the background, its diagonal pulses moving by redrawing a few tiles' graphics; and each voice is a spark that travels its own path along the fabric when it plays the figure, backwards when it plays the inversion. The counters measure every frame against the machine's limits. Play starts from the top with the machine's own chime; scrubbing is silent.
