The Atari 800XL on this page boots AltirraOS and Altirra BASIC, Avery Lee's
replacements for Atari's OS and BASIC ROMs (part of the Altirra emulator,
https://www.virtualdub.org/altirra.html). The files altirraos_xl.rom and
altirra_basic.rom here are the bytes of the C arrays the atari800 emulator
builds in (github.com/atari800/atari800, src/roms/, commit d722329), under
these notices:
