Kept in this browser, per system (the panel shows the game's). A gamepad works too: its d-pad or stick, its face buttons, Select and Start.

notes

The game stays on your computer: it is read by this page, never uploaded. Battery saves are kept in this browser (IndexedDB), per game. The ROTA splash plays before the game when ticked; any key skips it. reset presses the NES's reset button; the other systems have none (or none modelled), so their game starts again, its battery save written first and loaded again, without the splash.

The Game Gear, the Master System (with its FM unit), the Game Boy and Game Boy Color, the NES, the Super Nintendo, the Atari 800XL and CHIP-8 (the COSMAC VIP's) run from their models, written in Haskell and compiled to WebAssembly; the same models are the specification of an FPGA re-implementation. (The Super Nintendo draws its pictures on a second core of your computer, one frame behind the game; on a slow machine, frames that can't be shown aren't drawn. Its sound CPU boots our own boot ROM, not Nintendo's.) Source.

The Atari 800XL takes programs (.xex), cartridges (.car, or a raw .rom) and cassettes (.cas); without one, BASIC. It boots AltirraOS and Altirra BASIC, Avery Lee's free replacements for Atari's ROMs (their notice). Its keyboard is your keyboard, laid out as the 800XL's where it has the key: ' is +, \ is *, [ ] are < >, ` is ESC; F1 HELP, F2 START, F3 SELECT, F4 OPTION, F6 the inverse key, F8 BREAK; the arrows move the cursor. The joystick is on the keypad (8 4 6 2, 0 or right Alt the trigger) or a gamepad; F9 pauses.
